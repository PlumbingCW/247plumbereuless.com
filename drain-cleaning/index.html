Know the signs

Signs a drain clog needs a plumber

A single slow sink is often a hair or soap problem. When the symptoms spread or keep coming back, the blockage is usually deeper in the branch or main line, and a plunger will not reach it.

Gurgling after flushing

Air pushed back through a trap means the line downstream is partly blocked.

Several fixtures slow at once

A shower, toilet and floor drain that all drain slowly point to the main line.

Water backs up in a tub or shower

When the washer runs and the tub gurgles or fills, the shared drain is restricted.

Smell that returns after cleaning

Standing waste in a partial blockage keeps feeding the odor.

Act fast

When a clogged drain is an emergency

A clog turns into an emergency when wastewater is coming up into the house, when a toilet and a tub back up together, or when you cannot stop using water because of a family or a rental. Backed-up wastewater carries bacteria, so keep children and pets out of the area and avoid touching it with bare hands.

Stop using sinks, tubs and the washer, because every gallon you send down the line has nowhere to go. Our dispatcher can send a plumber the same day when one is available, including nights and weekends, and will tell you what to do until the truck arrives.

What to do while you wait

Turn off the washing machine and dishwasher, then stop flushing. Open a window if a sewage smell is strong, and move rugs and electronics away from the wet floor. If water is rising near an outlet, switch off that breaker only if you can reach the panel without standing in water.

Methods

How we clear it: snaking, cabling and jetting

The method depends on what is in the line and how far away it sits. A hand auger handles a trap or a short branch, while a motorized cable reaches farther and cuts through roots and dense buildup.

Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the pipe wall instead of poking a hole through the clog. We pick the lightest tool that will clear the line fully, because a hole punched through grease that is left clinging to the wall will close again within weeks.

Kitchen

Kitchen sink clogs that keep coming back

Kitchen drains clog differently from bathroom drains. Cooking grease cools inside the pipe and coats the wall, food particles stick to that coating, and the opening narrows a little more with every dinner.

If a snake only restores the flow for a few weeks, the line probably still holds a grease layer. A jetting pass, a lower-volume use of the disposal and scraping plates into the trash instead of the sink will extend the time between visits. A jammed disposal is a separate repair, covered on our garbage disposal page.

Beyond the clog

Drain line leaks and what a camera can show

Sometimes the water is not backing up from a clog but escaping from a cracked or separated drain line. A damp spot on the slab, a sewer smell with no obvious source or a drain that clears and slows again in a day all deserve a look with a camera.

In Euless, many drain lines run beneath slab foundations, so a failed joint is not visible and can saturate the soil beneath the house. A sewer camera inspection shows whether the pipe is blocked, offset or broken before anyone digs.
