Frozen Pipes in Euless, TX: How to Prevent and Respond to a Freeze

Hard freezes are rare in Euless, which is exactly why they cause so much damage. Many North Texas homes have lines in attics, garages and outside walls that were never built for sustained cold.

This guide explains how to prepare, how to thaw a frozen pipe safely and what to do when one bursts.

Prepare

Before the freeze arrives

Find your main water shutoff and test it, because you do not want to learn that it is stuck during a burst. Disconnect hoses, cover outside faucets and insulate exposed lines in attics, garages and outside walls.

Open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls so warm air reaches the pipes, and let faucets drip on the coldest nights. Moving water is harder to freeze, and the drip relieves pressure if ice does form.

Frozen

Signs a pipe has frozen

A faucet that gives only a trickle or nothing in freezing weather is the first sign. Frost on exposed pipe or a bulge in a pipe are other signs.

If you find one, open the faucet, shut off the water to that part of the house and start thawing gently before the ice splits the pipe.

Weak spots

Where pipes freeze first in a typical house

Lines in attics, garage walls and exterior walls are the usual victims, along with outside faucets and pipes in a crawl space. A kitchen sink on an exterior wall can freeze while an interior bath stays fine.

Walk the house before a freeze and list every spot where a pipe meets the outside. Foam pipe sleeves and caulk around openings where lines enter the house cost little compared with a flooded ceiling.

Thawing

How to thaw a pipe safely

Use a hair dryer on a low setting, a heating pad or warm towels, starting near the faucet and working toward the frozen section. Keep the faucet open so melting water can flow out.

Emergency

When a pipe bursts

Shut off the main water valve immediately, open faucets at the lowest point to drain the system and switch off power to any wet area you can reach safely. Photograph the damage and call a plumber.

A frozen pipe can split without leaking until it thaws, so the flood often starts when the weather warms. Our burst pipe repair page explains what a repair involves.

Do this first

Close the main valve, open the lowest faucet, move items off the wet floor and call the 24/7 line. If water is near an outlet or a panel, stay out and call the electric utility.

Afterward

Drying, documenting and repairing

Run fans, open windows if the weather allows and begin drying right away, since wet drywall and insulation grow mold within days. Photograph everything, keep the plumber's report and call your insurer.

After a freeze, check every outside faucet, attic line and garage pipe, even if nothing seemed to leak. Pipes can crack and only show it when water pressure returns. Leak detection helps if you suspect a hidden leak.

Checklist

A freeze-night checklist for Euless homes

  • Disconnect hoses and cover outside faucets.
  • Open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls.
  • Let faucets drip on the coldest night.
  • Locate the main shutoff and keep a wrench nearby if the valve needs one.
  • Know the 24/7 number to call if a pipe splits.

Keep the thermostat at a steady setting, even when you are away. A house that is allowed to chill can freeze pipes in the walls long before the living room feels cold, and a modest heating bill is cheaper than a flood.

Mistakes

Mistakes that make freeze damage worse

Using an open flame to thaw a pipe can start a fire in the wall. Another is turning the water back on at full pressure after a thaw without looking for splits, since a cracked pipe will flood when pressure returns.

Open the faucet and turn the main back on slowly, then walk the house and check every wall and ceiling near the pipes that froze.

Keep the 24/7 number saved in your phone before a freeze, since plumbers get busy fast when the temperature drops across the whole metroplex, and a call placed early in a cold snap is answered sooner than one placed at the peak.

Why it matters

What the 2021 storm taught Texas homeowners

The 2021 winter storm put long stretches of below-freezing weather over Texas, and burst pipes were widespread. It showed how many homes have supply lines in attics and outside walls, and how many homeowners did not know where their main shutoff was.

Euless notes on city emergency lines and shutoffs

The City of Euless public works utility division provides 24-hour service for after-hours and weekend emergencies, according to its public works FAQ, and the city is the right contact if a problem is on the city's side of the meter. Use the shutoff at your house to stop a leak on the private side.

Know which valve is yours before the weather turns. The valve at the street meter belongs to the utility, and the shutoff at the house is the one you should close.
