Privacy Policy

Effective 2026. This policy explains what information Euless 24/7 Plumbing handles when you visit this website or call our dispatch line.

Calls to our line

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your phone number, the time of the call and the details you share about your plumbing problem, such as your address and what is wrong.

Website information

This site does not have forms or user accounts. Our web hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, to keep the site secure and running. Embedded Google Maps and Google Fonts load from Google, which may receive your IP address under its own privacy policy.

How we use information

We use call details to understand the problem, dispatch a plumber and improve the quality of our service. We share the details a plumber needs to reach you and do the work.

Your choices

If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call. You can ask about information we hold by emailing contact@247plumbereuless.com.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the effective date when we do. Questions can be sent to contact@247plumbereuless.com.
