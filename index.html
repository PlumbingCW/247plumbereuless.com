When to call an emergency plumber in Euless

An emergency plumber is not only for floods. Call the 24/7 line when water will not stop, when sewage is backing up, when a water heater is leaking or when a pipe has burst and you cannot isolate it. A dripping faucet or a slow drain can wait for a daytime visit, but standing water on the floor cannot.

Our dispatcher asks a few questions and tells you what to shut off. A licensed plumber is then sent the same day when one is available, including after hours and on holidays, and the dispatcher explains any after-hours premium before you agree.

Water you cannot stop

Shut the main water valve, usually near the meter or in the garage, and call. See our burst pipe repair page.

Sewage or a backed-up drain

Stop using water, keep people away from the wet area and call. Our sewer line cleaning page explains the fix.

A leaking water heater

Shut the cold supply, switch off power or gas and call. Replacement is common when the tank itself leaks.

A gas smell

Leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Call us only after the utility says it is safe.

Euless, Texas

Plumbing in Euless homes, from Heritage Park to Glade Road

Euless is a Tarrant County city of about 60,000 people in the Mid-Cities, next to DFW Airport and crossed by State Highway 121 and State Highway 183. The city buys its main water supply from the Trinity River Authority, which draws from Cedar Creek and Richland Chambers reservoirs, and also runs city wells in the Trinity Aquifer, according to its public works page.

Where you live in Euless shapes the plumbing problems you are likely to meet, and it is worth reading each local detail with a practical question in mind.

Central Euless and Heritage Park

Heritage Park is a four-acre historic park with a barn, a brick home and a restored 1850s cabin. If you live in an older part of central Euless, ask your plumber what material your supply and drain lines are made of, because pipes in older houses behave differently from newer ones.

Glade Road and State Highway 121

Glade Parks and The Shops at Vineyard Village are open-air shopping centers along State Highway 121 in north Euless. Restaurants and shops in centers like these need after-hours help with grease lines and floor drains, which is what our commercial plumbing team handles.

Little Bear Creek and the park trails

The Preserve at McCormick Park and the Villages of Bear Creek Park lie along Little Bear Creek. Homes near creek corridors can have mature trees, and roots are a common cause of sewer stoppages, so a sewer camera inspection is useful after repeat clogs.

Clay soil under North Texas slabs

Most North Texas homes sit on slabs over clay that swells and shrinks with moisture. That movement can stress lines under the foundation, which is why a spinning meter or a warm floor spot calls for slab leak repair rather than a guess.
