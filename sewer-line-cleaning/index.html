Main sewer line backup: acting in the first hour

A main line blockage floods the lowest fixture first, which is usually a bathtub, a shower or a floor drain. Wastewater is unsanitary, so avoid contact and keep pets and children out of the area.

Shut off the washing machine and stop flushing, then call. Tell the dispatcher where the water is coming up and whether you can find a cleanout, because those details help the plumber bring the right tool.

Quick checklist

Stop all water use, find the cleanout cap and clear the path, protect electronics and rugs, and take a few photos of the affected areas for your records.

How it works

Cable or hydro jetting for a main line

A main-line machine pushes a heavy cable with cutter heads through the pipe. It chews through roots and breaks up compacted waste, and then a jet can wash the pipe wall.

We start with the lightest method that will clear the line. If grease is the cause, a cable alone leaves residue, and hydro jetting finishes the job. A camera confirms the line is open and shows whether a repair is needed.

Prevention

Stopping repeat sewer clogs

Most repeat main-line clogs have one of three causes: roots in a cracked joint, grease from the kitchen or flushed wipes. Each has a different fix, and cleaning without finding the cause means the line clogs again.

Flushable wipes do not break down like toilet paper, so they pile up at bends and joints. Keep only toilet paper in the toilet, put grease in the trash and consider a camera inspection if the clog returns within a year.

Septic note

City sewer versus septic

Properties inside city limits are generally on city sewer, and a clog in the private lateral is the owner's concern. If you have a septic tank, the symptoms and fixes differ, and pumping the tank may be needed instead of cleaning the line.

Tell the dispatcher which system you have when you call. We match the plumber to the job and will say plainly if a septic service is better handled by a specialist.

Follow-up

After the line is clear

A line that has just been cleared drains fast, which makes it a good time to run a camera along it. The video shows whether roots are returning at a joint or the pipe has a dip that holds waste.

If the camera shows a healthy pipe, you can go back to normal use and watch for early signs such as slow drains or gurgling. If it shows damage, the plumber explains whether lining, a spot repair or a section replacement fits, and the price is quoted before work begins.
