Where our 24/7 plumbers work

Our dispatch line is based in Euless, Texas, and covers Euless and the neighboring Mid-Cities towns. Euless is our home base, with ZIP codes 76039 and 76040, and the Euless homepage describes the plumbing issues common to the city.

Every service on our services page is available across the area, from drain cleaning and water heater repair to leak detection and sewer line repair. Emergency calls are handled around the clock, and the dispatcher confirms the address before a plumber is sent.

Euless, TX

Euless is a Tarrant County city of about 60,000 people, close to DFW Airport and crossed by State Highway 121 and State Highway 183. The city buys its main water supply from the Trinity River Authority, which draws from Cedar Creek and Richland Chambers reservoirs, and it also runs wells in the Trinity Aquifer, according to its public works page.

For water or wastewater questions on the city's side of the meter, the public works department is the place to start. For anything inside your property line, a licensed plumber is the right call, and our leak detection visit can show which side a leak is on.

Hurst, TX

Hurst has its own page because it shares a border with Euless and runs its own water and wastewater systems. The City of Hurst says it maintains about 205 miles of water lines and buys treated water through two connections with Fort Worth, and it also operates six city-owned wells.

See emergency plumbing in Hurst for ZIP codes 76053 and 76054, what the city says about who owns which part of the water line and what to do before the plumber arrives.

Bedford, Grapevine and North Richland Hills

We also take calls from Bedford ZIP codes 76021 and 76022, Grapevine ZIP codes 76051 and 76099 and North Richland Hills ZIP codes 76180 and 76182. Bedford sits north of Hurst and Euless along the same Mid-Cities corridor, Grapevine lies farther north near Lake Grapevine and State Highway 121, and North Richland Hills lies west of Hurst.

Each is a short drive from Euless, but arrival depends on the schedule, so the dispatcher confirms the address and tells you what to expect. We do not promise a specific arrival time.

How to tell us where you are

When you call, give the street address, the nearest cross street and the ZIP code.
