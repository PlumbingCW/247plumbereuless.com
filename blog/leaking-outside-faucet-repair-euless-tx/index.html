How to Repair a Leaking Outside Faucet in Euless, TX

An outside faucet that drips is easy to ignore, and that is the problem. A small drip wastes water all season, and a leak inside the wall can rot framing before you see a stain.

This guide shows how to find where the water is coming from, what you can fix with a wrench and when the pipe behind the faucet needs a plumber.

Find the leak

Where is the water coming from

Open the faucet and watch each spot. A drip from the spout when the handle is closed points to a worn washer or seat inside the valve. Water at the base of the handle when the faucet is on means the packing nut has loosened or the packing has worn.

Water that shows up inside the house, behind a wall or under a nearby sink is a different problem. That usually means the pipe behind the faucet has split, often after a freeze, and the leak only appears when the faucet is turned on.

Simple repairs

Fix a drip, a loose handle or a worn washer

Shut off the supply to the outside faucet if there is a valve inside the house, then open the faucet to drain. If there is no valve, you may need to shut the main water valve first.

1

Tighten the packing nut

A quarter turn often stops a leak at the handle.

2

Replace the washer

Remove the handle and stem, then fit a matching washer.

3

Wrap the threads

Use plumber's tape on the faucet threads for a tight seal.

4

Test

Turn the water on slowly and watch for drips.

If the faucet is old, corroded or pitted, replacement is often faster than repair. A frost-proof model is a good upgrade, because it puts the shutoff inside the heated part of the house.

Freeze damage

Signs of a split pipe behind the faucet

Water expands when it freezes, and a hose that stays attached holds water in the faucet. The ice can split the faucet or the pipe behind it, and the break often shows up only when warm weather returns and the water is turned on.

Wet wall or ceiling near the faucet

The pipe has split inside the wall.

Water bill jump

Water is running into the wall cavity.

Faucet sprays at the wall

The break is close to the outside wall.

Mold smell in the garage or room

Water has been leaking for a while.

If you see any of these, shut off the water to that faucet and call a plumber. Our faucet and fixture repair team can open the wall, repair the pipe and replace the faucet, and leak detection helps if the source is unclear.

Before winter

Protect outside faucets from freezing

Disconnect hoses before the first hard freeze, drain the faucet and cover it with an insulated cap. If there is an interior shutoff, close it and open the outside faucet to drain the pipe.

North Texas winters can swing from mild to hard freeze in a day, and the 2021 winter storm showed how many exposed lines can fail at once. A few minutes with a cap and a wrench in the fall is cheaper than a wall repair.

Replacing

Choosing a replacement faucet

A frost-proof sillcock has a long stem that puts the valve seat inside the warm part of the wall, and it should slope slightly downward so water drains out. A vacuum breaker built in prevents water from flowing back into the house supply from a hose.

Matching the size and type matters. Most are three quarter inch hose threads with a half inch pipe connection, but threaded, sweat and push-fit versions need different tools.

Checklist

A quick checklist for a leaking hose bib

  • Find the interior shutoff for the line, if there is one.
  • Check the wall and ceiling nearest the faucet for stains or soft spots.
  • Tighten the packing nut a quarter turn before replacing anything.
  • Replace the washer or the faucet if the drip continues.
  • Fit a vacuum breaker if the faucet does not already have one.

The order matters. Cheap fixes come first, and the moment you see water inside the house the job changes from a repair to an investigation.

A hose bib that has leaked inside the wall for a season can leave rot behind drywall and insulation, so look at the room side of the wall after any leak before you close the question.

Mistakes

Mistakes that turn a drip into a wall repair

Leaving a hose attached through a freeze is the biggest one. Another is ignoring a faucet that only leaks when it is on, because the leak may be inside the wall where you cannot see it.

A third is wrapping the problem in tape or sealant rather than finding the source. A patch on a split pipe hides the leak without stopping it, and the wall pays for the delay.

Local notes

Meter box, shutoff and who owns what

Know where your own shutoff is before you need it. The valve at the street meter belongs to the utility, so use the shutoff at the house to stop a leak.

Euless notes on water lines and freezes

The City of Euless public works water and wastewater division is responsible for testing and maintaining the city's water lines, according to the city. A leak on your side of the meter is yours to repair, and if you are unsure where the boundary sits, ask the division about your address.

The City of Hurst, which borders Euless, states that it owns the line to the meter and the customer owns the line from the meter to the house, which is a useful reference for how many utilities draw the line. A plumber can test the private side with a pressure test and mark the leak.
