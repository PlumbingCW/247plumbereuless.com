Before we arrive

Steps that limit water damage in your home

These steps apply to any house, and they cost nothing. Do them before you call if water is running where it should not.

1

Shut off the water

Close the main valve or the fixture shutoff under the sink or behind the toilet.

2

Turn off the water heater

For an electric heater, switch off its breaker. For gas, set the control to pilot or off.

3

Protect the floor

Move rugs and electronics, and put towels or a bucket under any active drip.

4

Take photos

A few photos of the leak and the damage help with an insurance claim.

5
