The area

What the Villages of Bear Creek is

The City of Euless lists Villages of Bear Creek Park at 1951 Bear Creek Parkway, Euless, TX 76039, and the park shares its address with the city's off-leash dog park. The name carries into the neighborhoods around it. Real estate listings show subdivisions called Estates at Bear Creek, Villas at Bear Creek and Dominion at Bear Creek, and Homes.com describes the Estates at Bear Creek as a gated new-construction community.

The area is not one uniform block of houses. The same name is attached to single-family subdivisions and to rental communities such as The Enclave at Bear Creek, an apartment community listed on East Ash Lane. We do not know exact boundaries, so if you are unsure whether your address counts, give the street and ZIP code when you call.

Housing and plumbing

Houses, townhomes and apartments need different calls

Who fixes what depends on how you live. A homeowner in a single-family house owns the plumbing from the meter inward. A renter in an apartment usually reports a leak or a stopped drain to the property manager first. A homeowner in a community with an association should check which pipes the association covers before booking a repair.

Water heaters

Newer homes and tankless water heaters

Some recent listings around Euless mention tankless water heaters as a feature of newer construction. A tankless unit is a different repair from a tank, with its own venting, gas line and descaling needs. If you have one, read our tankless water heater page. If you have a tank, water heater repair covers the usual causes of no hot water or a leak at the base.

Before you call

What to have ready for a plumbing call

Find the main shutoff

Locate the water shutoff valve for your home or unit. In an apartment it may be inside a utility closet.

Note the symptom

Write down what you see, such as water at the base of a toilet, slow drains in more than one fixture or no hot water.

Check gate and access

If your street is gated, have the gate code or a contact ready so the plumber can reach the door.
