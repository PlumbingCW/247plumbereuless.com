The area

Midway Park and the streets around it

The City of Euless lists Midway Park at 300 West Midway Drive as a 22-acre linear park with 0.8 miles of trails, baseball fields, picnic areas and playgrounds. It is also the home of the Euless Family Life Center and its adjoining aquatic park. Redfin groups homes near the park and the Euless Rec Center under a Midway Park neighborhood, and most of those listings are single-family houses in Hurst-Euless-Bedford ISD.

Listing records use Midway Park and Midway Square as subdivision names, with addresses on West Harwood Road, East Midway Drive and Crane Drive. We have not found an official boundary, so we describe the area by the park and these streets.

Housing

Older central Euless and what that means for plumbing

A Homes.com guide to Euless says many homes from the 1960s through the 1980s are ranch-style and concentrated in the center and south of the city, and that the citywide median year built is 1984. That is a statement about the city, not a survey of Midway Park, and we do not know the age or pipe material of any particular house here.

The practical point is that a home you have owned for years deserves a periodic look at the parts that wear. Water heaters, supply shutoffs, toilet internals and drain lines all have limited lives, and a plumber can tell you what yours are.
