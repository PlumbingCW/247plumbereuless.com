Commercial plumbing emergency: what to do first

Shut off the water to the affected area, or the building main if you cannot isolate it, and move stock and equipment away from the water. Switch off power to a flooded room only if you can reach the panel safely.

Then call the 24/7 line. A tenant should also notify the landlord or property manager, since the lease usually decides who authorizes the repair, and the dispatcher can explain what the plumber will need from you.

Business owner checklist

Close the nearest shutoff, photograph the damage for your insurer, move inventory off the floor and note the time the problem started. Keep a record of who you spoke to and when.

Grease

Grease traps and kitchen drain care

A grease trap catches fats before they reach the sewer, and it needs regular pumping or cleaning to work. A full trap lets grease pass into the line, which leads to backups and possible city enforcement.

Ask Euless public works about the grease rules that apply to your business, because requirements vary by use and size. Regular hydro jetting of kitchen lines complements trap cleaning by washing off the pipe wall.

Planned work

Scheduling work around business hours

A water heater swap, a new sink or a drain line replacement can often be done early in the morning or after close, so the shop stays open. Tell the dispatcher your hours and which fixtures you cannot lose.

A short walk-through with the owner or manager before a big job helps the plumber find shutoff valves, check access above ceilings and plan around deliveries.

Compliance

Backflow testing and storm drains

Many water utilities require commercial backflow assemblies to be tested on a schedule, because a failed assembly can let contaminated water flow back into the supply. Ask the City of Euless public works department what applies to your meter, and we can arrange a test by a qualified tester.

Storm drains and area drains at the back of a lot need to stay clear before heavy rain. A jetting visit clears debris and silt so water leaves the site instead of flooding a loading dock.

Leak detection

Leaks, high bills and honest limits

A commercial water bill that rises with no change in use points to a hidden leak, and our leak detection methods apply to small and mid-size buildings. Large multi-story systems, boiler plants and industrial process piping are outside what a dispatch line can promise, and we will say so on the call so you can line up a specialist.

Planned work such as a plumbing installation for a build-out or a water heater replacement can be scheduled outside business hours to avoid downtime.
