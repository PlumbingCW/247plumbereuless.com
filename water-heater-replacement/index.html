Failed tank

Water heater failed: what to do tonight

A tank that is rusting through or seeping from the shell cannot be repaired. The steel liner has corroded, and the fastest way to stop damage is to shut off the cold-water valve above the heater and switch off power or gas.

Call us after you have stopped the supply. The dispatcher will ask about the age, fuel type and tank size so the plumber can bring the right unit, and we can often replace a standard 40 or 50 gallon tank in a single visit when stock is available.

Stop the damage first

Close the cold supply valve, turn off the breaker or gas control, and set a bucket under the drain valve. Hose the water to a floor drain if the tank is leaking heavily, and keep the area clear of children and pets until the plumber arrives.

Sizing

Choosing the right size and type

A tank that is too small runs out of hot water, and one that is too large wastes fuel keeping extra gallons hot. A plumber sizes the heater by household count, the number of bathrooms and how many fixtures run at once.

HouseholdTypical tank sizeNotes
1 to 2 people30 to 40 gallonsSingle bath, light laundry
3 to 4 people40 to 50 gallonsMost common size for a family home
5 or more people50 to 75 gallonsMultiple baths, heavy simultaneous use

These are common starting ranges rather than a rule. The first-hour rating on the heater label matters more than the tank volume for busy mornings.

What a swap includes

What a replacement visit covers

1

Drain and disconnect

Water, gas or power and the old tank are cleared away.

2

Check the fittings

Shutoff valves, supply lines and venting are inspected.

3

Set and connect

The new tank is set in a pan with a drain line to a safe place.

4

Test and show you

Pressure, venting and temperature are verified and explained.

A good replacement also replaces worn flex connectors and the relief valve, adds a drain pan where the heater sits above living space and checks whether an expansion tank is needed. Ask the City of Euless building department about permit rules for your address, because local requirements can change.

Upgrade options

Stay with a tank or move to tankless

A like-for-like tank swap is the fastest and usually the cheapest repair after a failure. A tankless unit costs more up front and may need a bigger gas line and new venting, but it removes the tank that can leak and heats water as you use it.

If you want to compare, our tankless water heater page explains the trade-offs. If you are replacing the tank as a planned upgrade and not after a failure, the installation page covers what to expect.
